# PRD — Digital Industrial Workflow System (DIWS)

## Product Summary
DIWS is a multi-tenant, AI-enabled SaaS platform for manufacturing companies to manage procurement, inventory, production, sales, dispatch, documents, reports, and RAG-based knowledge access in one system.

## Problem Statement
Manufacturing companies often use disconnected tools for stock, production, documents, and reporting. This causes delays, manual work, poor visibility, and weak decision-making.

## Goals
- Digitize core factory workflows
- Support multiple companies on one platform
- Keep data isolated per company
- Enable company-specific AI assistance using RAG
- Provide a scalable foundation for future modules

## Target Users
- Company Owner
- Company Admin
- Factory Manager
- Production Planner
- Warehouse Manager
- Purchase Manager
- Sales Executive
- Dispatch Manager
- Accountant
- AI Assistant User

## MVP Scope
- Company registration and login
- RBAC and multi-tenancy
- Company/factory/warehouse/product management
- Supplier/customer management
- Inventory, procurement, production, sales, dispatch
- Reports and dashboard
- Document upload and search
- AI assistant with RAG

## Out of Scope (Version 1)
- IoT integration
- Workflow builder
- Mobile app
- Advanced finance/accounting
- Customer/supplier portals
- Predictive maintenance
- RFID/PLC/SCADA

## Success Metrics
- Faster order-to-dispatch workflow
- Reduced manual stock tracking
- Better visibility of production and inventory
- Useful AI answers from company documents
